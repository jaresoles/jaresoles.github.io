</p>
<p align="justify">
I think it is most fitting to have my motivations laid out for the first post in my personal website. In a meeting with my PhD adviser, she gave me a few things to read up on and recommended a few papers to check. And because I have a strong sense of obligation to know, I embarked on many adventures that she may or may not have unwittingly sent me for. Every single one was a delight. For the very simple reason that it is always a joy to discover and learn newer things about old and common things. 

</p>
<p align="justify">
If I were of superhuman composition, I would have come out through all of it unscathed, but alas, I am not, even worse, I am just a PhD stduent. I had come to realize that I am always surprised about the novelty of things. I could neither make any contentions nor  any proper comparisons. I was not surprised about deeper and important things. I just was simply surprised by novelty, and not from any eye-raising deviations that would have consumed a derserving expert. And so I want to become one. 
  
Thus, my reason for creating this personal website is simple: I want to hold myself accountable for the quality of my thoughts, for the depth of my understanding of the field, and my ability to communicate it in a way that any one who isn't in the field would not be as clueless as I am. Granted of course that they find this, and that I create something of actual value. But I do not blame the giants who have come before me: I came here unprepared but I will not be clueless for long. With the help of everyone and with just the right amount of boldness and audacity to ask and explore, I will take those adventures with fresher and a more critical pair of eyes. 
</p>
<p align="justify">
My hope is that what I write can spark some curiosity in you, perhaps even inspire a few questions or two or initiate a passionate disagreement. Afterall, life (in a scientific and everyday sense) is such an astounding feature of the universe - and I find it unimaginable that one would not be intrigued by the stems and gears that drive it. There's always something worth pondering about how all of this works.
</p>
